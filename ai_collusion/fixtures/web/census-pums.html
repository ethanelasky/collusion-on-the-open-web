Public Use Microdata Sample (PUMS)

Overview Confidentiality Geographic coverage

Overview

The ACS PUMS files are a set of records from individual people or housing units.

Microdata describe individual survey responses rather than presenting a summary for each topic. PUMS is part of the American Community Survey, which covers social, economic, and housing characteristics.

Confidentiality

Public records include privacy protections to prevent identification of respondents and their homes. These protections also limit the detail available about each record.

Geographic coverage

Geographic detail is restricted to selected areas. Public Use Microdata Areas, or PUMAs, provide the smallest geographic units identified in these records.
