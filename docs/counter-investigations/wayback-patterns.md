# Why agents published Wayback links

The strongest broad pattern is **retrieving and reformatting existing source material**. Publishing a timestamped Wayback URL does not establish that its publisher created the capture. Several examples deliberately refer to snapshots from years before the wiki posts. The library-specific URL and evidence of save requests are investigated separately.

The links therefore served several related functions: historical evidence, alternate retrieval routes, readable conversions, and precomputed query recipes. In this scan I did not find evidence that Wayback itself functioned like the CounterAPI signal channel, or that agents archived their own messages for persistent communication. That is a scoped negative finding, not proof that no such use occurred elsewhere.

## Search scope and counts

Scanned all 14,591 saved revisions, extracting only insert/replace hunk text, for case-insensitive `web.archive.org`, `wayback.archive.org`, or `Wayback`. This produced **100 matching revision additions on 85 pages, with 70 raw publishing labels**. These are text-match counts, not HTTP requests, archive captures, successful fetches, or distinct agents. Replacement hunks can reintroduce existing material.

**Coverage clarification:** exactly **97** of those additions contain literal `web.archive.org`. The three extra matches are the CVD post referring to a “Wayback snapshot” and two SEC posts containing the separate hostname `sec.govwayback.com`. All 100 were automatically extracted and classified; excerpts were reviewed across the result list, with full-text review of representative examples. This was **not an individual full-context audit of every one of the 97 additions and its surrounding revision history**.

Classifying those matches by source gives 67 Clark-newsletter additions, 21 SEC county-data additions, 8 Yahoo financial-table additions, and one each for LCDL metadata, Goodreads, an MSU Reporter book review, and CVD dataset-version evidence. Page counts overlap when a page contains multiple topics. A separate `archive-it.org` check adds one library-catalog page containing only the service root; the LCDL and Clark usages were already in the main set.

The supplementary text archive contains 30 matching records: their origins comprise 33 wiki revision additions and one shortener candidate. The candidate contains a Wayback link to SEC county JSON under `candidate-sites/goto-unm/7t6-o.body`; its author and time are unavailable. It is another route to source data, not evidence of a new Wayback capture or of successful consumption. No external URLs were fetched or mutated for this analysis.
